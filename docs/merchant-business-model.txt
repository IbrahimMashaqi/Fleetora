1. CORE BUSINESS MODEL
Fleetora is a multi-tenant delivery-company management SaaS for Palestine.
Three connected products share the existing central NestJS backend and PostgreSQL database:
the existing company web dashboard, a merchant mobile app, and a later-phase driver mobile app.
There is no customer mobile application.
The MERCHANT creates parcels from the merchant mobile application. The delivery company sees
them automatically and manages pickup, custody, assignment, delivery, returns, COD, and settlement.
The company need not manually recreate merchant parcels.
Each merchant belongs to exactly ONE delivery company; a company has many merchants.
Merchants never choose a company when creating parcels. Resolve company securely through
authenticated merchant membership. Isolate both company and merchant. No merchant multi-company
membership in Phase 1.

2. MERCHANT CREATION, INVITATION, AND ONBOARDING
The company initiates onboarding: authorized company administrator/employee creates merchant
business information and primary pickup address, assigns a commercial pricing plan, issues secure
activation invitation. Merchant owner accepts, activates account, authenticates in merchant app,
and manages merchant parcels and authorized merchant employees.
No random public registrations may attach themselves to a company.
Reuse existing authentication. Invitation tokens: cryptographically secure, single-use,
time-limited, bound to merchant/company/invited identity/intended role, server validated,
revocable, replay/cross-tenant safe. Store hashes, not raw secrets. Do not expose tokens in logs
or unrelated API responses. States pending, accepted, expired, revoked.
If delivery provider is missing, offer honest manual invitation issuance to authorized admins;
never fake email/SMS delivery. Do not rewrite or bypass Better Auth.

3. MERCHANT USERS AND EMPLOYEE PERMISSIONS
One merchant business may have multiple individual authenticated people.
Owner: view all own parcels; create/edit/cancel eligible parcels; bulk import; pickup requests;
track pickup/delivery/returns/exchanges; view statements/balances; request settlement;
manage employees/permissions; view merchant activity/audit.
Employee permissions individually separate: view parcels, create, edit, cancel, bulk import,
create/manage pickups, view returns/history, view financial statements, request settlement,
manage users (owner-level unless safely delegated explicitly).
Ordinary employees receive NO automatic balances/statements/payout requests/employee admin.
Enforce permissions in NestJS. Audit individual actor for creation/edit/cancellation/financial
actions. Company staff roles remain separate. No parallel conflicting authentication system.

4. MERCHANT ADDRESS AND PICKUP LOCATIONS
No merchant branches. One primary business address and default pickup address/location.
Each pickup request can use default location, alternate address, or alternate map pin where
supported. Request-specific location never changes primary address or creates a branch.
Different requests may have different addresses; company sees exact request location.

5. ZONE-BASED DELIVERY PRICING
Company controls fees by destination zone, merchant plan, merchant-specific discounts,
approved promotions/offers, and contractual terms effective at confirmation. Palestine, initial
default ILS. Company-scoped zones must support neighborhoods/localities within a city, not only
one city price. Clear zone identity, validated selection. No invented prices/boundaries/addresses.
Each merchant has an active same-company commercial plan. Plans determine zone fees,
discounts/special prices, approved offers, fee payer/settlement rules, relevant commercial terms.
Only appropriately authorized company actors change assignments/plans. Backend-centralized
pricing engine with unit tests, never duplicated formulas in dashboard/mobile.
Confirmed parcels store immutable zone/base fee/discount/promotion/final fee/fee payer/plan
identity/version or equivalent audit context. Future tariff/plan edits never rewrite snapshots.
Post-pickup amendments require explicit auditable workflow.

6. CRITICAL FINAL CUSTOMER PRICE RULE
Merchant enters FINAL amount customer must pay; it ALREADY INCLUDES DELIVERY.
Never add company delivery fee on top. Final 150 ILS, fee 15 ILS -> collect exactly 150,
expected merchant proceeds 135 assuming merchant-funded delivery and full successful collection.
Merchant pricing preview: final payable, contractual delivery fee, expected merchant proceeds
subject to collection/charges. Backend authoritative, exact minor units/decimal, no float money.
ZERO-COD/PREPAID: final payable ZERO allowed (prepaid/gift/no collection). Zero does not imply
free delivery. Charge merchant according to plan through proper accounting entry, never invent
customer payment. Financial recognition and cash movement remain separate.

7. PARCEL CREATION — MERCHANT MOBILE APP
Collect recipient name, phone, optional alternative phone, delivery city/zone, detailed address,
optional map pin, final payable, COD/no-COD classification, description, package/piece count,
delivery notes, optional merchant reference and necessary existing validated fields.
Generate unique tracking identity. Associate authenticated merchant, its company, creating user.
Appear in correct company dashboard automatically. Creation is NOT physical company receipt:
clearly pending pickup/receipt. Real persisted data, no fake activity.

8. PARCEL EDITING AND CANCELLATION
Before physical receipt, permitted merchant users edit eligible information/cancel directly,
with audit. After company/driver receipt, material amendments need company approval;
address/zone/price changes recalculate fees and expose financial consequences before approval.
Cancellation becomes a request requiring controlled custody/return workflow.
After delivery, never overwrite finalized financial/delivery facts; explicit adjustments/returns/
corrections. Record actor/time/old+new values/reason/approval where relevant.

9. BULK PARCEL IMPORT
Individual and bulk creation. Supply Excel template; accept Excel upload; parse/validate each
row server-side; validate ownership, tenant, names/phones/zones/final prices; apply plan pricing;
preview row errors/calculated fees; confirm valid parcels; persist with tracking numbers;
honest completed/rejected/duplicate counts. Individual/bulk labels. Idempotency/import tracking
prevents retry duplicates. No silently invalid creation or false all-success on partial failure.

10. BARCODE / QR LABELS
Unique tracking identity; QR/barcode label generation/printing. Codes may identify a parcel,
but never expose unprotected customer personal/financial data. Scan is neither authorization
nor custody proof. Physical handoffs require receipt/confirmation.

11. PICKUP REQUESTS
A: company pickup. Group multiple created parcels into ONE request. Merchant chooses ready
parcels, preferred day/window, default/alternate address/pin, notes. Window is preference, NOT
confirmed appointment. Company reviews, confirms/proposes alternative, assigns authorized
pickup driver. Distinguish accepted/proposed/pending slots. Record actual received parcels,
including missing/discrepant items.
B: merchant warehouse drop-off. Merchant selects drop-off; authorized company warehouse staff
confirms physically arrived parcels. Original creation never proves receipt.
C: direct merchant-to-customer delivery allowed after actual pickup; warehouse not mandatory.
Currently one warehouse; schema allows later expansion, no merchant-branch subsystem.

12. DRIVER-TO-DRIVER HANDOFF RESTRICTION
No direct driver-to-driver handoff. Reassignment passes through documented authorized company
custody/warehouse. No transfer feature. Driver mobile remains later-phase.

13. MERCHANT APP — PHASE 1 SCREENS
Professional Flutter + Dart merchant app at apps/merchant_app. Use feature-oriented
modules, Riverpod, go_router, Dio, typed serialization, platform secure token storage,
Flutter gen-l10n, Material 3 and MapLibre. No direct database access or imported NestJS
implementation. Backend APIs remain authoritative for permissions and money.
Screens: activation/login; home; list/search; create; edit eligible parcel; details/timeline;
pricing preview; bulk import workflow; create pickup; pickup list/details; account/profile;
owner employee management. Arabic/English and RTL; real loading/error/empty/retry states;
no fake activity/success.

14. COMPANY DASHBOARD INTEGRATION
Reuse Next.js operations dashboard. Display merchant-created parcels, merchant/recipient,
destination, FINAL collection amount, contractual fee, pickup method, pending requests,
confirmed receipts, status/history. No second parcel admin system. Protect company-only actions.

FUTURE COMPATIBILITY ? APPROVED LATER DOMAINS, NOT PHASE 1 IMPLEMENTATION
The subsequent Flutter master specification preserves these later business requirements:
- One main company cashbox and one per driver; multiple company bank accounts.
- Daily driver cashbox statements, partial/full remittances and cashier confirmation
  before posting cash transfers; no spending directly from driver COD cashboxes.
- Positive/negative merchant balances; cash/bank payouts, company expenses, driver
  salaries, configurable commissions, advances, daily closing and discrepancy review.
- Free/fixed/zone-based return fees, distinct failure fees, merchant attempt policies,
  loss/damage investigations, exchanges and merchant-configured inspection rules.
- No partial acceptance of an individual parcel's contents.
- Per-company WhatsApp Business, customer status notifications and company inbox.
- Future driver app call/WhatsApp/navigation controls, route planning and GPS only
  through secure driver authentication. No driver app implementation in Phase 1.
Direct driver-to-driver handoff remains prohibited; reassignment requires documented
company custody procedures. These requirements do not authorize activating future domains.
